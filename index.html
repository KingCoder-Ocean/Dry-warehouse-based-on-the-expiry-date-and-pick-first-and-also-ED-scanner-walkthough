Dry store, 3D walkthrough

3 aisles, 6 racks (A to F), 79 bays. Goods are placed by how fast they sell: fast movers near the cross aisle on the floor shelf, slow movers at the far ends and on the top shelves. It also shows the scanning plan: a fixed QR label on every shelf, handheld scanners for staff, fixed scanners at goods in and dispatch, and a best-before date for every pallet. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to move.

Where scan data goes

Handheld or fixed scanner
Reads the shelf label and the pallet label. Stores nothing itself; sends over Wi-Fi.
▼
WMS (warehouse system)
Main record of every scan: who, when, shelf, product, cases, batch, best-before date.
▼
Seasons (ERP)
Stock levels update automatically, so office and warehouse agree.
▼
Azure
Keeps the full history for reports and forecasting.
▼
Power BI dashboards
Stock going out of date in 30, 60 and 90 days; pick accuracy; stock levels.
